# 📊 Indonesia Socio-Economic Data Analysis & Visualization

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes and visualizes regional socio-economic data across provinces in Indonesia using **Tableau**. The dashboard presents several socio-economic indicators through interactive visualizations to explore regional differences, compare conditions across provinces, and identify relationships between key indicators.

The visualizations transform socio-economic data into clear and informative insights using maps, charts, and comparative visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze socio-economic conditions across provinces in Indonesia.
- Explore the distribution of poverty across regions.
- Compare Gross Regional Domestic Product (GRDP) between provinces.
- Examine differences in per capita expenditure across regions.
- Explore the relationship between poverty and per capita expenditure.
- Compare poverty conditions with life expectancy across provinces.
- Present regional socio-economic information through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🗺️ Poverty Map by Province

A geographical map visualizes the **average poverty percentage** across provinces in Indonesia, allowing users to explore regional differences in poverty levels.

### 🧩 Poverty Distribution by Province

A visualization presents the **distribution of poverty across provinces**, providing another perspective for comparing regional poverty conditions.

### 🫧 Poverty and Life Expectancy

A bubble-based visualization compares **poverty percentage** with **life expectancy** across provinces.

The visualization uses bubble size to represent differences between provinces and helps explore patterns between these socio-economic indicators.

### 📊 GRDP by Province

A horizontal bar chart compares the **average Gross Regional Domestic Product (GRDP)** across provinces.

The visualization highlights differences in regional economic output and allows provinces to be compared based on their average GRDP.

### 📈 Poverty and Per Capita Expenditure

A combined line chart compares:

- **Poverty percentage**
- **Average per capita expenditure**

across provinces.

This visualization provides a way to explore differences between poverty conditions and household expenditure across regions.

## 📈 Visualization Approach

The dashboard uses several visualization techniques to present the socio-economic data:

- **Map** for geographical comparison between provinces.
- **Bar Chart** for comparing GRDP across provinces.
- **Bubble Chart** for exploring poverty and life expectancy.
- **Line Chart** for comparing poverty percentage and per capita expenditure.
- **Interactive Visualizations** for exploring regional socio-economic conditions.

These visualizations provide multiple perspectives for understanding differences in socio-economic indicators across Indonesia.
